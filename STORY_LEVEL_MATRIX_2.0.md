# Rule Garden: The Last Promise — Story-to-logic matrix

The logic is derived from the story conflict. No lesson exists merely to introduce a logic family.

| # | Scene and dramatic conflict | Why this logic is necessary | Character movement | Consequence and reveal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Bridge of Embers.** The Welcome Flame is gone and the bridge can support only returning ember-bearers. Fear and noise are unreliable. | A single positive condition creates a clean tutorial and establishes that surface drama is not the cause. | Pip equates usefulness with being correct immediately. | Correct crossings preserve the first Heart embers; silver ash proves theft. |
| 2 | **The Wardroot's Two Wounds.** Missing magic makes wardroots fail through either dim light or cracked bark. | OR logic is required because two independent symptoms justify the same repair. | Juniper teaches Pip to observe living evidence rather than demand one tidy sign. | The failures are spreading through the valley's defenses. |
| 3 | **The Rootgate Breach.** Keepers and medics both need access to pursue the thief. | Two valid paths establish that more than one kind of service can earn trust. | Rowan favors control; Juniper argues for multiple legitimate forms of service. | The thief used an old service route known only to senior apprentices. |
| 4 | **Two Sparks to Name a Ghost.** The forge can reveal the ash signature only with a stable minimum charge. | Numeric threshold distinguishes “almost enough” from operationally sufficient. | Lumi recognizes Sable's craft and chooses concealment. | Sable's apprentice seal appears; Rowan withholds motive. |
| 5 | **The Glassmaker's Oath.** Living glass must be warned before a person crosses it. The same actions reversed shatter the pane. | Sequence logic is the physical law of the scene. | Lumi's divided loyalty deepens; Sable's message attacks blind obedience. | The pursuit moves onto Tovin's old route. |
| 6 | **The Second Morning.** Silver ash must persist across two mornings to distinguish Sable's trail from ordinary forge soot. | Memory across time is the only reliable evidence. | Pip learns that present evidence can be incomplete without history. | Sable appears and tells the first half of the Black Rain story. |
| 7 | **Care Before the Flame.** Juniper is hurt while a stolen Flame slides toward danger. | Ordered multi-action priority expresses the thematic choice: person before property, then necessary work. | Pip proves he can learn compassion; Sable's certainty cracks; Juniper reveals she was the child Tovin saved. | The old Gate's moral failure becomes personal and visible. |
| 8 | **Voices in the Moonhouse.** Tovin's echo needs a strong voice by day, but loud sound wakes lethal lantern moths at night. | Context changes the correct action without changing the visitor. | Rowan confesses; Lumi admits helping Sable; Sable hears the truth and escalates. | Tovin gave away his mark; Rowan knew the exception was missing. |
| 9 | **The Stolen Flames.** The Unbinding scatters Flames near similar-looking channels. | Relational matching is necessary because neither rune alone proves the correct channel. | Sable sees her plan harming residents but refuses to restore centralized authority. | Most Promises can be recovered; the last Flame remains with Sable. |
| 10 | **The Echo Procession.** Sable creates repeating copies of allies to delay pursuit. | Comparison with the immediately previous arrival detects true changes rather than fixed traits. | Lumi rejects passive admiration and actively outthinks Sable. | The real route to the Heart is found. |
| 11 | **Before the Black Rain.** Living arches overheat, but imminent rain will safely cool them. | Threshold plus exception prevents both neglect and harmful overreaction. | Juniper entrusts Pip with a decision affecting the whole valley. | The path survives as the Wilddark reaches Mossgrove. |
| 12 | **The Last Promise.** Sable is injured, authorized helpers arrive, and unstable arrivals threaten the chamber. | Ordered three-action priority is the story's moral architecture: urgent care, stable help, then restraint. | Pip becomes the Listening Keeper; Sable changes course; Rowan relinquishes power. | The old system is replaced by community-held, revisable Promises. |

## Escalation contract

- Act I moves from immediate survival to discovery of a deliberate saboteur.
- Act II moves from pursuit to moral rupture and revelation of institutional failure.
- Act III moves from system collapse to a permanent political and emotional resolution.
- The visual consequence of each failure differs: bridge collapse, wardroot blight, stranded helper, forge backlash, glass fracture, false trail, abandoned injury, moth swarm, crossed Flames, echo loop, scorched arch, Heart breach.
- Earlier characters and ideas return in the finale; the last level is not an isolated “hard puzzle.”
